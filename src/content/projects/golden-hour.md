Golden Hour is a single-page web experience designed as a digital moment of calm. Built around a breathing sunset gradient with animated bokeh particles and a pulsing sun, it offers two simple interactions:

## Features

- **New Poem** — Generates unique 3-line poems from a curated word bank. Each combination is procedurally generated and lines fade in sequentially with smooth animations.
- **Vibe Chord** — Selects a guitar chord (G, D, C, Em, Am, F, Dsus4, Cadd9, G7, A7), describes the mood it carries, and softly arpeggiates it through the browser's speakers using the Web Audio API.

## Design Philosophy

No ambient drone (by design), no accounts, no tracking. Just a sunset, some words, and a chord. The interface uses glass-morphism cards, warm sunset gradients, and elegant typography (Cormorant Garamond + Outfit) to create an atmosphere of intentional slowness.

## Use Cases

- Play alongside it on guitar while it cycles through chords
- Screenshot a poem that resonates
- Leave it running on a second screen while writing or working
- Use the chord suggestion as a jumping-off point for new progressions
