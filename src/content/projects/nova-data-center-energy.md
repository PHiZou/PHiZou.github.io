# NOVA Data Center Energy Usage

This project explores how data center growth is reshaping energy demand, water use, and infrastructure pressure in Northern Virginia.

## What I built

- Demand-growth timeline and capacity views
- Regional facility mapping
- Grid-stress and energy-source views
- Water-use and economic context sections
- A scrollytelling structure that ties the whole story together

## Why it matters

The underlying data lives across utility filings, planning documents, and public reports. This project pulls those pieces into one clearer, interactive narrative.

## What this project shows

- strong D3 and interactive visualization work
- ability to synthesize many public data sources into one usable story
- interest in infrastructure and regional systems
- judgment about how to explain complex topics without flattening them
